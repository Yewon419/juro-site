Juro Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Juro is operated by Windgarden. Contact windgarden419@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Sign in with Apple identifier: the user identifier Apple gives the app. We do not ask for your name or email.
  • Display name: a generated nickname at first, which you can change.
  • Race records: the days you enter, the activity you pick (reading, work, study, creating), when you start and stop the timer, and the group you are placed in.
  • Friends and friend races: your friend code, friend requests and acceptances, and friend races you join.
  • Cheers: the preset messages and moves you send and receive.
  • Blocks and reports: who you block, and who you report and why.
  • Push notification token.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or your use of other apps. Juro contains no analytics or advertising tools.

How we use it

Only to form groups and rank races, run friends and friend races, send notifications (9 AM start, 9 PM results, friend race invites), and handle reports and blocks.

What other people see

Your display name, chosen activity, records, rank and the cheers you send are visible to people in your group, in friend races you share, and to your friends. Your friend code is visible only to you.

Service providers and transfers

  • Supabase Inc. (USA) runs the database and sign-in server. Data is stored in the Seoul region.
  • 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo, USA) delivers push notifications. Each notification sends your push token and the notification text to servers in the USA.
  • Apple Inc. (USA) handles Sign in with Apple and push notifications (APNs).

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. Tap “Delete account” at the bottom of the Friends screen and it is removed from the service database at once; this cannot be undone. Any backup copies held by our hosting provider expire with that provider’s backup retention.

Deleting the app alone leaves your information on the server. If you cannot use the app, email windgarden419@gmail.com to ask for deletion.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or stop processing your information. You can change your name in the app; send any other request to windgarden419@gmail.com.

Children

Juro is not directed at children under 14. If we learn we hold information from a child under 14, we delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we post it here with a new effective date.
