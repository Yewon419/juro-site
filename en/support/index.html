Reporting

You can report someone from their row in the race results room, from a runner’s chip during a race, or from the friend menu. Reporting someone also blocks them.

We review reports within 24 hours and act on inappropriate names or behavior, for example by changing the name or deleting the account. You can also report by email.

Frequently asked questions

  • When is the race? Every day from 9 AM to 9 PM. Pick an activity before 9 AM to enter that day’s race.
  • How is time counted? You start and stop the timer yourself while you do productive work. Juro does not monitor your phone; it runs on the honor system.
  • What does blocking do? From the next 9 AM group draw you are not placed in the same group, and you no longer see their cheers.
  • How do I delete my account? Tap “Delete account” at the bottom of the Friends screen. It is deleted immediately.
